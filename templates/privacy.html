General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Compliant Self-Hosted Policy

1. Overview and Data Controller

OpenWebTTS is a self-hostable open-source software application. This privacy policy explains how personal data is collected, used, and stored when you use this particular installation.

Data Controller: The individual or organization deploying and running this instance acts as the Data Controller (under Art. 4(7) GDPR) for data processed on this server. The original software creators and upstream contributors do not collect, receive, or process your personal data from self-hosted instances.

2. Categories of Data Processed

Depending on how you use this instance and whether you create an account, the following data may be processed:

  • Account Data: Username, securely salted and hashed password, account creation timestamp, and session security tokens.
  • User Content: Text inputs, saved books, uploaded documents, audiobooks, reading progress, and custom bookmarks stored in your user profile.
  • Audio and Voice Cloning Data: Uploaded audio samples for voice cloning, synthesized speech audio files, and temporary audio cache.
  • Preferences: User interface settings (theme preference, font choice, highlight colors, TTS chunk size, voice mappings).
  • Technical & Log Data: Basic HTTP request logs (IP addresses, user agent, timestamp) standard to web servers for security and operational monitoring.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

We process your personal data only when there is a lawful legal basis:

  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): To provide text-to-speech synthesis, manage your library, and maintain your account requested by you.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Where you choose to upload voice samples for voice cloning or connect optional external cloud services with your own API credentials.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): To ensure server security, prevent abuse or denial of service, debug errors, and maintain system performance.

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • Local Hosting: By default, all data is stored directly on the storage volume of this host server.
  • Audio Cache: Synthesized audio chunks are cached locally for performance and may be purged by the user or administrator at any time.
  • Retention Period: Account data and saved library materials are retained until you delete them or your account is removed by the instance administrator.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

OpenWebTTS does not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or telemetry beacons.

  • Local Storage: Used strictly for functional purposes, such as storing local reader state, volume/speed settings, and active preferences.
  • Session Authentication: Cryptographically signed session tokens are used solely to keep you logged in to your account.

6. Third-Party Data Disclosures

OpenWebTTS is designed for local privacy-respecting computation. When using local synthesis models, no audio or text leaves this host server.

Data is only transmitted externally if you or the administrator actively enable external integrations:

  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech / Gemini API: If selected, text prompts are transmitted to Google Cloud APIs using your configured service account.
  • Hugging Face: When downloading community voice models from the interface, requests are made to Hugging Face model repositories.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (Articles 15-22 GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Request confirmation and access to the personal data held about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate personal data or update your library items.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17): Request deletion of your account, saved books, uploaded audio samples, or cached files.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18): Restrict processing of your personal data under certain statutory conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Receive your stored personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise these rights, please contact the administrator or hosting provider of this OpenWebTTS instance.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

The instance administrator may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect configuration changes, software updates, or legal compliance requirements.
